Reliability & Recovery

Designed for the day something fails.

A commercial edge system needs a practical recovery path—not just good intentions.

Off-Site Backups

Nightly compressed database backups are pushed off-site and logged for success or failure.

Known-Good Images

Recovery images support rapid replacement without rebuilding the software stack from scratch.

Managed Services

Application services start on boot and restart automatically if the process exits.

Remote Diagnosis

Private access, logs, health indicators, and backups allow many incidents to be diagnosed remotely.
